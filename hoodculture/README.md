# HoodCulture Website + Dukaan Store

## How it works
- This website = your brand lookbook & product showcase
- **Real orders & payments** happen on Dukaan: https://mydukaan.io/hoodculture

## Buy buttons
All "Shop Now", "Buy", and "Buy on Store" buttons open your Dukaan store.

## Admin
Open `admin.html` (password: hoodadmin) to manage local demo products.

## Files
- `index.html` – Homepage
- `shop.html` – Product showcase
- `product.html` – Product details
- `admin.html` – Local admin panel
- Official store: https://mydukaan.io/hoodculture
